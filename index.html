How coverage is determined: platforms come from the "Desktop / RWD Tablet / RWD Mobile" prefix on each issue's Summary; an unprefixed issue is counted for the platform of the CSV it came from. Each row above is one Success Criteria (aggregated across all its axe Auditor checkpoints); expand it to see exactly which pages they are present on, and on which platforms. This reflects where an issue is present, not full test coverage — a platform showing as absent may mean it was tested and found clean, or that it wasn't covered by the uploaded CSVs. Watch for a ⚠ on a page (spelled differently across files) or a checkpoint-count badge (an SC covering more than one distinct checkpoint, which can have different platform coverage from each other).
